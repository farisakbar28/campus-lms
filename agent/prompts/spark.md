# Prompt — SPARK

```
Decision needed: <the choice to make>

Context:
- Constraints: production VM 1 GB RAM, $0 budget, no credit card, laptop
  8-16 GB, solo developer, 12-week timeline.
- Goals: production-grade system AND my genuine understanding of it.

Give me 3 options. For each:
- How it works, in three sentences
- What it costs: complexity, RAM, money, learning time
- What it makes easy later, and what it makes hard later
- Who uses this in industry, and at what scale it starts to matter

Then a recommendation, explicitly labelled RECOMMENDATION, with the single
strongest argument against it.

Finish with what I should write in the ADR's Context and Options sections —
but leave the Decision to me.
```
